## The problem
A long list of job-search results can create more work without making the decision clearer. Titles vary, requirements need interpretation, and a recommendation needs evidence.

## My extension
Built on the open-source Career Ops framework, JobFit AI separates discovery, triage, fit evaluation, gap analysis, recommendation, and the person's final application decision.

- Web-candidate intake with canonical URLs and source provenance.
- Liveness checks and deduplication before deeper review.
- A 0–100 Fit Score kept separate from Gap Severity and Recommendation.
- Source-linked resume drafts with document rendering and approval state.
- Workflow and scanner checks in the public repository.

## What I am exploring
How to evaluate an AI-assisted pipeline rather than judge it by the quality of one answer. My work includes failure analysis, rubric calibration, and human labeling before revealing the system's judgment.

## Scope
The public project extends an existing framework. User profiles, applications, and generated personal documents belong in a private user layer. A fresh clone needs its own configuration; scanning alone does not complete evaluation or submit applications.

## What this demonstrates
Evidence-aware decision support, evaluation contracts, human review, and the practical boundary between finding a lead and deciding it is a fit.
