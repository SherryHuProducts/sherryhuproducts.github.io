## The case
An independent, synthetic construction-finance case study from the perspective of a Project Accountant. It is a learning project, not a record of professional data-center delivery.

## Current work
The project setup and formula-driven GMP baseline establish the initial financial model. I am developing the connected cost-control and reporting workflow around it.

## Design scope
Budget and cost codes, commitments, change orders, invoices, retainage, actual-cost reconciliation, Estimate to Complete, Estimate at Completion, and project reporting.

These describe the case study's intended coverage, not a claim that every module is complete. Cost controls and forecasting remain in development.

## The question behind the model
How can finance and project teams share a clear view of the baseline, approved changes, committed costs, and the expected final outcome?

## Data boundary
All project, vendor, contract, invoice, and financial data is fictional. No employer or confidential business data is used.
