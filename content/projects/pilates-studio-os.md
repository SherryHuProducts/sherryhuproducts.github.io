## Why this domain
I teach Pilates, and I know how much lesson preparation depends on scattered movement notes, teaching cues, student context, and previous sessions. This project connects that domain knowledge with software design.

## What exists today
Product requirements, system architecture, the domain model, a PostgreSQL / Supabase database foundation, schemas and migrations, movement templates, and an initial Reformer Essential movement library.

## What I am building toward
A workflow in which an instructor reviews a student's goals and history, retrieves appropriate teaching knowledge, creates a lesson, adjusts it, and records feedback afterward.

## Current boundaries
The retrieval layer, REST API, AI lesson-planning workflow, instructor application, and end-to-end testing are still planned. The current phase is knowledge modeling and movement-library construction.

## What this demonstrates
Turning domain expertise into data relationships and product requirements, while keeping the instructor's judgment central to the eventual experience.
